Roadmap

From zero to pipeline in 90 days.

How we go from kickoff to a live outbound system generating meetings with your ideal prospects.

01 Week 1–2

The Foundation

We build the sending infrastructure from scratch: hundreds of email accounts across purpose-built domains, allocated across dedicated IPs and warmed before a single message goes out. In parallel, we work through your ICP with you, define the segments worth testing, and write differentiated messaging for each one. By the end of week two, the system is live.

02 Week 3–4

Launch & Grow

Initial outreach launches and meetings with ideal prospects often start booking immediately. Early performance data and engagement insights lay the groundwork for optimization and scale.

03 Month 2

Optimize & Scale

With real response data in hand, we double down on high-performing sequences, refine messaging based on prospect engagement, and scale winning campaigns to new segments and larger lists.

04 Month 3

Systematize & Expand

The system matures into a predictable pipeline generator. We document the playbooks, expand to additional prospect segments, and hand over a process your team can run and own independently going forward.
